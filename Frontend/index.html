NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

A few quick answers about HVAC Connect and how the platform supports your operations.

Still have questions? Contact us →

HVAC Connect is a centralized operations and integration platform designed for HVAC, refrigeration, facility management and industrial service organizations.

The platform is intended for organizations and teams that coordinate customers, vendors, sites, assets, contracts, technicians and service operations.

Core capabilities include vendor and customer onboarding, site and asset management, contract administration, service ticket tracking, SLA monitoring, inventory control and reporting.

The platform is designed around multi-site operational visibility. Contact the team to discuss your organization's specific location and workflow requirements.
